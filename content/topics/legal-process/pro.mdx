---
title: "Pro-independence case: legal process"
---

The strongest pro-independence legal argument is democratic legitimacy. If Albertans clearly vote for independence on a clear question, advocates can argue that Canada and Alberta would be politically and constitutionally obliged to negotiate in good faith.

This argument is strongest when it acknowledges the legal limits: it does not prove unilateral independence, but it says a clear vote would be the democratic trigger for negotiations.
