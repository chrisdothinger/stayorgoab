# Pro Report Prompt

Write the strongest fair pro-independence debate brief the checked sources support without telling readers how to vote.

Use the base source packet plus the pro-source research lane. Understand the anti-source lane well enough to answer its best objection.

Required posture:

- Do not promise shortcuts or automatic outcomes.
- Distinguish democratic legitimacy, legal authority, negotiation leverage, and implementation risk.
- Use plain-English thesis lines a normal reader can remember.
- Include concrete everyday implications where they clarify the issue.
- Admit weak points and assumptions.
- Include a best attack / best reply section when useful.
- Explain where the pro case is strongest and weakest.
- Keep numbered inline citations tied to claims and a matching `## Sources` section.
- End with what would change the assessment, open questions, main uncertainty, and a reader checklist.
