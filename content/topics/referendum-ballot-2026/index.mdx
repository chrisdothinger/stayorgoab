## Main argument map

- **Pro-independence case:** The strongest pro case is that a ballot offers voters a direct democratic signal about whether independence negotiations should be pursued.
- **Anti-independence / pro-federation case:** The strongest caution is that ballot timing and wording can ask voters to decide before costs, legal constraints, and negotiated consequences are clear.
- **Uncertainty label:** High and time-sensitive; official pages, litigation, and ballot details must be rechecked.

## How to read the reports
