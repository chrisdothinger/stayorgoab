---
title: "Neutral synthesis: legal process"
---

The legal baseline is narrower than many political claims. A provincial vote can matter politically, but it does not directly create a new state.

The Supreme Court of Canada said a clear democratic expression in favour of secession would create a constitutional duty to negotiate. That does not predetermine the outcome of negotiations. The Clarity Act gives the House of Commons a role in deciding whether the question and result are clear enough for federal negotiations.

For readers, the most important distinction is between a referendum as democratic evidence and independence as a constitutional/legal outcome. StayOrGoAB should treat claims that skip the negotiation and amendment stage as unsupported unless they are tightly qualified.
