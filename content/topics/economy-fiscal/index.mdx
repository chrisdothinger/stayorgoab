## Short answer

No current official source can prove that independence would automatically make Alberta richer or poorer. The answer depends on assumptions about revenues, expenditures, debt, services, trade, federal transfers, and negotiations.

## What we know

Alberta publishes budget and fiscal plan documents. These are the right starting point for current provincial revenue, expense, capital, and debt context.

## What is disputed

Advocates disagree about whether Alberta would keep more revenue, face higher transition costs, or lose fiscal advantages from being inside Canada.

## What remains unknown

A future independence settlement would need decisions about debt allocation, federal assets, program replacement, taxation, trade arrangements, and currency.
