## Short answer

Indigenous rights and treaties are not side issues. The leading Canadian secession reference says Aboriginal interests would need to be taken into account in constitutional negotiations.

## What we know

Section 35 rights and treaty concerns make this a high-risk topic. The Supreme Court's Secession Reference specifically acknowledges Aboriginal interests in a secession context.

## What is disputed

Parties disagree about whether an Alberta independence process could proceed, what consultation would require, and how treaty relationships with the Crown would be affected.

## What remains unknown

Alberta-specific litigation and political responses could materially change the practical status of this topic.
