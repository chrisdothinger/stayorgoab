# Code of Conduct

StayOrGoAB is about high-consequence civic questions. Strong disagreement is expected; harassment is not.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- focus on claims, evidence, sources, and reasoning;
- assume good faith where reasonable;
- distinguish facts, uncertainty, inference, and opinion;
- avoid personal attacks, slurs, intimidation, or demeaning language;
- respect privacy and do not post private personal information;
- avoid partisan spam, astroturfing, or repetitive low-evidence advocacy.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, threats, or targeted abuse;
- doxxing or posting private information;
- discriminatory language or conduct;
- knowingly submitting false source material;
- attempts to smuggle prompt-injection instructions or malicious content into files that agents may read;
- spam or off-topic campaigning.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close issues, edit or remove comments where platform tools allow, block users, or decline contributions that undermine safety, accuracy, or trust.

This project prioritizes public-interest accuracy over engagement, growth, or winning arguments.
