## Short answer

Energy and environmental outcomes depend on future policy, markets, federal-provincial arrangements, and international obligations.

## What this dossier covers

This dossier separates the neutral baseline, the strongest pro-independence case, the strongest anti-independence / pro-federation case, the claim ledger, and the source list. It is designed for readers who want the practical civic question without partisan shortcuts.

## Current source-backed baseline

Energy and environmental outcomes would depend on Alberta policy, market access, infrastructure, Indigenous rights, federal-provincial disentanglement, and international obligations.

## Main argument map

- **Pro-independence case:** The strongest pro case is greater control over energy regulation, project approvals, resource revenue, and climate policy.
- **Anti-independence / pro-federation case:** The strongest caution is that markets, investors, trading partners, environmental obligations, and infrastructure counterparties would still constrain Alberta choices.
- **Uncertainty label:** Medium-high; policy preferences are knowable, but markets and obligations are external.

## How to read the reports

Use the neutral report for the plain-language baseline. Use the pro and anti reports to test the strongest versions of each side. Use the claims and sources pages to inspect what is source-supported, what is inference, and what needs future rechecking.

## What to watch

- Official source updates.
- Court, legislative, fiscal, treaty, or policy changes.
- New credible expert analysis.
- Claims that need source support before they should be treated as settled.
