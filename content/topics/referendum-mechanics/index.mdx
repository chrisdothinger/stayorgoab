## Short answer

The independence petition and the 2026 referendum context are related but not the same thing. Elections Alberta publishes current petition status, initiative process rules, and referendum information.

## What we know

Elections Alberta lists the independence citizen initiative petition, its signature threshold, and the May 2, 2026 end of signature collection. Elections Alberta also says a referendum has been set for October 19, 2026.

## What is disputed

Political disagreement surrounds whether petition success, referendum framing, and legal challenges should lead to a separation question, another referendum question, or no referendum on independence.

## What remains unknown

The exact ballot question and legal status may depend on Orders in Council, petition certification, litigation, and future official updates.
