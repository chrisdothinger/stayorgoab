## Core argument

This synthesis compares the pro and anti cases rather than creating a third campaign position. The pro-independence side
generally points to control, mandate, leverage, or policy flexibility. The anti-independence/pro-federation side generally points to
transition risk, unresolved legal or fiscal questions, and the need for proof before replacing existing
arrangements. For this topic, the decisive issue is not whether one side can imagine a better or worse future;
it is whether source-backed evidence has narrowed uncertainty around petition validity, referendum wording,
timing, litigation, and later political decisions.
