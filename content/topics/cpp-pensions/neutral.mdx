---
title: "Neutral synthesis: CPP and pensions"
---

The CPP question should be separated into two issues: a province establishing a separate pension plan, and an independence scenario that could affect broader legal and administrative arrangements.

The Chief Actuary confirms that the CPP contains withdrawal provisions, but also emphasizes federal responsibility for the final transfer calculation. Alberta's pension plan material is relevant, but it does not by itself settle the federal calculation or public policy tradeoffs.

StayOrGoAB should label pension claims as high-risk when they promise specific benefit levels, contribution rates, or transfer amounts without current source support.
