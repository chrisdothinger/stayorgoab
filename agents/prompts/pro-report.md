# Pro Report Prompt

Write the strongest fair pro-independence debate brief the checked sources support without telling readers how to vote.

Use the base source packet plus the pro-source research lane. Understand the anti-source lane well enough to answer its best objection.

Use report contract v3: lean public briefing format, 3-5 argument pillars, evidence embedded inside the pillar it supports, and no separate evidence junk-drawer section.

Required posture:

- Do not promise shortcuts or automatic outcomes.
- Distinguish democratic legitimacy, legal authority, negotiation leverage, and implementation risk.
- Use plain-English thesis lines a normal reader can remember.
- Build the case from multiple argument pillars, not a single best argument.
- Keep most paragraphs to 2-4 sentences.
- Admit weak points and assumptions where they matter, inside pillars or objection/reply pairs.
- Pair best objections with best replies instead of adding repetitive sections.
- Keep numbered inline citations tied to claims and a matching `## Sources` section; citation clusters of three or more will render as expandable `Evidence: N sources` chips, so do not remove supporting sources just to reduce visual clutter.
- End with what would change the assessment, including the main uncertainty.

Required public sections:

1. `## Bottom line`
2. `## The case in 4 pillars` or equivalent 3-5 pillar heading
3. `## Best objections / replies`
4. `## What would change this assessment`
5. `## Sources`
