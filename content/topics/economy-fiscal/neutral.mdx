---
title: "Neutral synthesis: economy and fiscal policy"
---

The credible fiscal starting point is Alberta's current budget and fiscal plan. Independence claims that jump directly to household gains or losses need clear assumptions.

The pro-independence case usually emphasizes control over revenue and policy. The pro-federation case usually emphasizes transition risk, federal program replacement, market uncertainty, and negotiation uncertainty.

StayOrGoAB should not publish a single net-dollar claim unless the calculation is sourced, assumptions are visible, and uncertainty is plain.
