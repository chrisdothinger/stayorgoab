## Short answer

There is a statutory path for a province to leave the CPP and establish its own plan, but the financial transfer, transition rules, and independence-related pension outcomes are not settled by current public sources.

## What we know

The Chief Actuary has published a position paper on subsection 113(2) of the Canada Pension Plan. Alberta also maintains public pension information and Alberta Pension Plan material.

## What is disputed

The central dispute is how to calculate any CPP asset transfer and whether an Alberta plan would be better, worse, or riskier for contributors and beneficiaries.

## What remains unknown

Independence would add additional negotiations about citizenship, residency, portability, administration, and federal-provincial obligations.
